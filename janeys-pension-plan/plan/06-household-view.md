## What this tells us

1. **The two State Pensions get you to "Minimum" on their own** (~£24,500/year), and
   Janey's small pot nudges you just over it. That's a solid, guaranteed floor.
2. **The gap to "Moderate" (~£16k/year) is Michael's private pension's job.** This is
   why his pot and contributions are the key missing numbers — they'll likely move the
   household from "Minimum-plus" toward "Moderate". Add them to see the real picture.
3. **Securing both State Pensions in full is still the top priority.** Between them they
   provide ~90%+ of the current modelled household income — so Janey's forecast/NI gaps
   and confirming Michael's £241 figure both matter a lot.
4. **The £500k mortgage-free home is a backstop, not income.** It doesn't pay the bills
   month to month, but it underwrites later-life options (downsizing, or as a reserve).
