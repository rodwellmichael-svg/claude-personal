## What this tells us

1. **The household is in decent shape.** ~£33,800/year (~£2,818/month) sits comfortably
   above the couple **"Minimum"** and is within striking distance of **"Moderate"** — and
   that's *before* adding Michael's private pension.
2. **Guaranteed, inflation-linked income dominates.** Two State Pensions (~£24,500) plus
   Janey's Teachers' Pension (~£7,000) give ~£31,500/year of secure, index-linked income
   for life. That's an unusually solid, low-risk base.
3. **Michael's private pension is the swing factor for "Moderate".** The ~£9,300/year gap
   to Moderate is roughly what his pension needs to provide. Adding his numbers will show
   whether the household clears it. As a rough guide, ~£9,300/year of drawdown implies a
   pot of ~£230k (at 4%) — so his figures really matter here.
4. **The £500k mortgage-free home is a backstop, not income** — it underwrites later-life
   options (downsizing, reserve) rather than paying monthly bills.
5. **The bridge years are well covered.** Janey's Teachers' Pension (£6,982 from age 60 +
   £20,945 lump sum) bridges the gap before the State Pensions start.
