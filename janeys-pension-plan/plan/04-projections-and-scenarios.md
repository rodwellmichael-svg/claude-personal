## How the model works

Starting pot **+** annual contributions (grown each year at the assumed return, net of
charges) **→** projected pot at retirement **→** converted to income via a sustainable
withdrawal rate **or** an annuity, **+** State Pension from State Pension age.

Results are shown in both **future money** and **today's money** (inflation-adjusted).
