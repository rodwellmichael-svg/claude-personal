# Janey's Pension Plan

A personal UK retirement-planning project for **Janey**: understanding where she
stands today, what income she'll need in retirement, and a clear, adjustable plan
to close any gap.

## What's here

| Item | Where | What it is |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Plan** | [`plan/`](plan/) | A written retirement plan in parts: inputs, a UK pensions primer, current position, strategy, projections, a household view, and an action checklist. |
| **Projections** | [`janeys-pension-projections.xlsx`](janeys-pension-projections.xlsx) | An adjustable model (Inputs, Projection, Retirement income, Household, Timeline, Retire 60 vs 65, Comfortable-plus tabs): contributions → growth → pot at retirement → sustainable income, with scenarios, a Janey+Michael household summary, a year-by-year timeline, an early-retirement comparison, and a Comfortable-plus scenario including Michael's NEST pot. Edit the blue/yellow cells on the **Inputs** tab and everything recalculates. |

## Important note

This is **planning and educational material, not regulated financial advice**.
UK pension rules, allowances, and tax treatment change and depend on personal
circumstances. Figures are illustrative until Janey's actual numbers are entered,
and any decision should be checked against current [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/browse/working/state-pension),
[MoneyHelper](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/), and — for anything significant —
a regulated financial adviser (find one via [FCA](https://register.fca.org.uk/) /
[Unbiased](https://www.unbiased.co.uk/)).
