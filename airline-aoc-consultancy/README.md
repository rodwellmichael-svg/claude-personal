# AeroCert Partners — Airline AOC Consultancy

A business-creation plan for a specialist aviation consultancy that helps
start-up and expanding operators obtain and maintain **UK CAA** and **EASA**
Air Operator Certificates (AOCs), together with the associated Operating
Licences and continuing-airworthiness approvals.

> **Working name:** *AeroCert Partners* — a placeholder used throughout this
> plan. Replace with the final trading name once incorporated.

## How to use this plan

1. **Validate the numbers.** Section 6 uses illustrative figures. Replace them
   with quotes from your own accountant, insurer, and first real pipeline.
2. **Confirm current regulation.** Section 3 summarises the framework as a
   guide for structuring services — it is **not** legal advice and rules
   change. Always work from the live UK CAA and EASA / National Aviation
   Authority (NAA) source documents on any real engagement.
3. **Adapt the roadmap.** Section 9 assumes a founder-led launch with a small
   associate network. Scale the timeline to your funding and headcount.

## Regulatory disclaimer

This material is a commercial planning aid. It is not legal, regulatory, or
financial advice, and it does not create any relationship with the UK Civil
Aviation Authority, EASA, or any National Aviation Authority. Certification
requirements are set by those authorities and are updated regularly; verify
every requirement against current published sources before relying on it.
