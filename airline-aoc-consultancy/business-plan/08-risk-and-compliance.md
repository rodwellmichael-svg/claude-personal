# 08 — Risk & Compliance

Risk management for a certification consultancy is itself a selling point:
clients are buying our judgement about *their* risk, so our own governance must
be exemplary.

## Risk register (summary)

| # | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|------|--------|-----------|-----------|
| R1 | **Reputational** — a botched or delayed programme | Severe | Low–Med | Peer review, hard qualification, honest scoping, never over-promise on authority-controlled timelines |
| R2 | **Professional liability** — client claims loss from our advice | Severe | Low | Robust PI insurance, clear engagement terms, documented advice, scope boundaries (Section 2) |
| R3 | **Fixed-fee overrun** | Med–High | Med | Tight scoping, explicit assumptions, formal variation process |
| R4 | **Client insolvency / bad debt** | Med–High | Med | Qualification, deposits, milestone billing, stop-work rights |
| R5 | **Key-person dependency** on founder | High | Med (early) | Build 2nd senior consultant + associate bench; document the IP |
| R6 | **Demand volatility** (lumpy pipeline) | Med | High | Grow recurring Tier 3 to cover fixed cost; diversify segments |
| R7 | **Regulatory change** devalues/reshapes services | Med | Med | Continuous regulatory monitoring; turn change into new service lines |
| R8 | **Confidentiality / data breach** | Severe | Low | NDAs, access control, information-security discipline |
| R9 | **Associate quality / availability** | Med | Med | Vetting, peer review, back-to-back terms, a deep enough bench |
| R10 | **Conflicts of interest** (competing operators) | Med | Med | Conflict checks, information barriers, transparent disclosure |

## Legal & contractual foundations

- **Engagement letters / MSAs** with clear scope, assumptions, exclusions,
  liability caps, and a variation mechanism.
- **Professional Indemnity insurance** sized to the sector and to the largest
  programme value — a launch prerequisite, not an afterthought.
- **Associate agreements** — back-to-back confidentiality, IP assignment, PI
  cover, and no-poach.
- **Corporate structure** chosen with the accountant/lawyer to ring-fence
  liability appropriately.

## Ethical guardrails

- **We are advisors, not the operator's certificate.** We never present a
  guarantee of certification or of an authority-controlled timeline.
- **Honest counsel.** If an operator is not ready or not viable, we say so —
  even at the cost of the sale. This protects the firm's most valuable asset:
  its reputation with the authorities and the market.
- **Postholder integrity.** Any interim/fractional postholder arrangement must
  be genuine, properly structured, and acceptable to the authority — never a
  paper appointment.

## Compliance-monitoring of ourselves

Treat the firm like a regulated organisation: a light internal quality-
management system, periodic self-audit of engagements against our own
methodology, and a lessons-learned loop feeding the findings playbook.
