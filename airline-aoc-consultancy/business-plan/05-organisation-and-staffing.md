# 05 — Organisation & Staffing

## Guiding principle

**Small permanent core, large flexible bench.** Fixed payroll is the enemy of a
project business with lumpy demand. Carry only the roles that must be
always-on; buy everything else from the associate network per engagement.

## Core team (launch)

| Role | Responsibility | When to hire |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| **Founder / Managing Consultant** | Sales, senior authority relationships, programme oversight, quality sign-off | Day 1 |
| **Lead Certification Consultant** | Runs programmes day to day; owns the document factory | Day 1 or first big win |
| **Programme Coordinator / Analyst** | Project admin, compliance-matrix upkeep, client cadence, document control | Once 2+ concurrent programmes |
| **Business Development / Ops Manager** | Pipeline, proposals, contracts, finance admin, associate management | Year 1–2 as revenue supports |

A credible **founder with genuine certification track record and authority
relationships** is the single most important asset. The whole go-to-market
rests on that credibility (Section 7).

## The associate bench

Recruited and vetted ahead of need, engaged per project:

- **Ex-authority / senior ops managers** — flight ops, ground ops, training.
- **Airworthiness & CAMO partners** — continuing airworthiness, MEL, Part-145
  interface.
- **Type & special-approval specialists** — PBN, LVO, ETOPS/EDTO, dangerous
  goods, HEMS/HHO/offshore.
- **Interim / fractional postholders** — where properly structured and
  authority-acceptable.
- **EASA member-state specialists** — local NAA knowledge for EU-based
  certifications.

**Bench management:** back-to-back contracts (confidentiality, IP assignment,
PI insurance, no-poach), a skills/availability matrix, and a fair, transparent
rate card so the best associates stay loyal.

## Advisory / non-executive

- A **non-exec with airline C-suite or ex-authority standing** for credibility
  and door-opening.
- Trusted **legal** (aviation/corporate) and **accounting** partners for the
  economic-licensing and structuring workstreams.

## Culture & standards

- **Reputation-first.** Every deliverable is authority-grade; we never ship
  work we would not defend in an audit.
- **Independence & ethics.** We advise honestly even when the answer is "don't
  proceed yet."
- **Knowledge compounding.** Every engagement feeds the template library and
  the findings playbook.

## Indicative scaling

- **Year 1:** 1–2 core + 4–6 active associates.
- **Year 2:** 3–4 core + 8–10 associates.
- **Year 3:** 5–6 core + 10–15 associates, with a retained-compliance book that
  covers fixed overhead before any new certification sale.
