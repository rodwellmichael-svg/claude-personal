# 04 — Operations & Delivery Model

The consultancy runs on three pillars: a **repeatable methodology**, a
**document factory**, and an **associate network**. Together they let a lean
core team deliver large, complex programmes with predictable quality.

## 1. The engagement lifecycle

```
Enquiry → Scoping → Proposal → Kickoff → Delivery (5 phases) → Handover → Retained
```

1. **Enquiry & qualification** — is this a real, funded, credible operator?
   Qualify hard; certification work with an under-capitalised client is a
   reputational and cash-flow risk.
2. **Scoping** — apply the five-axis scoping model (Section 3.7) to size the
   programme. Often delivered as a paid **Tier 1** engagement.
3. **Proposal** — fixed scope, milestone-based fee, assumptions, and explicit
   authority-controlled dependencies.
4. **Kickoff** — appoint the programme lead, agree the RACI, set up the shared
   workspace and the five-phase plan (see the template in `/templates`).
5. **Delivery** — run the five certification phases; weekly client cadence and
   a live findings/action log against the authority's feedback.
6. **Handover** — final documentation set, a compliance register, and a
   post-certification obligations briefing for the client's management team.
7. **Retained** — convert to a Tier 3 compliance retainer.

## 2. The document factory (core IP)

Our efficiency lever is a **library of authority-tested manual frameworks**:

- Master templates for OM Parts A–D, SMS/Compliance Monitoring, MEL, and
  training documentation, structured to the current rule references.
- A **compliance matrix** mapping each regulatory requirement to the manual
  section and evidence that satisfies it — this doubles as the authority
  submission index.
- A **findings/response playbook** capturing common authority findings and
  proven responses.

Each engagement improves the library (with client-specific content stripped
out), so **every programme makes the next one faster**. Protecting and version-
controlling this IP is a strategic priority.

## 3. The associate network

The core team stays small; specialist and surge capacity comes from a
**curated bench** of vetted associates:

- Ex-authority inspectors and senior operational managers.
- Type specialists, training/checking experts, airworthiness/CAMO partners.
- Interim/fractional postholders (where the arrangement is properly structured
  and authority-acceptable).

Associates are engaged per-project under back-to-back terms (confidentiality,
IP assignment, PI cover, and no-poach). This keeps fixed cost low and margin
flexible (see Section 6).

## 4. Quality, IP, and information security

- **Peer review** — no manual or authority submission leaves the firm without a
  second senior reviewer.
- **Single source of truth** — one controlled client workspace; strict version
  control on every document.
- **Confidentiality** — clients share commercially sensitive plans; robust NDAs
  and access control are non-negotiable.
- **IP protection** — the template library is the firm's crown jewel; associate
  and employee agreements must assign IP and protect the library.

## 5. Tooling

- Project & document management (controlled workspace, versioning, audit trail).
- The template library and compliance-matrix tooling.
- CRM for the pipeline (Section 7) and a knowledge base of regulatory updates.
- Time and margin tracking per engagement to keep fixed-fee work profitable.
