# 07 — Go-to-Market

## Positioning statement

> **AeroCert Partners gets operators to a UK or EASA AOC faster and with less
> risk — senior certification expertise delivered as a managed, fixed-scope
> programme.**

In a trust-driven, referral-heavy market, the brand must signal **credibility,
seniority, and safety** above all. This is not a volume, price-led business.

## Brand pillars

1. **Authority-grade rigour** — everything we produce would stand up in an
   audit.
2. **Dual-jurisdiction fluency** — UK *and* EASA as a core competence.
3. **Predictability** — clear scope, milestones, and price; no surprises.
4. **Founder credibility** — real certification track record and authority
   relationships front and centre.

## Channels (in priority order)

1. **Founder network & referrals.** The primary channel. Certification is
   bought on trust and reputation. Prioritise warm introductions from lessors,
   lawyers, financiers, OEMs, and past colleagues.
2. **Ecosystem partnerships.** Referral relationships with:
   - Aviation **law firms** (they do the corporate/economic side, we do ops).
   - **Lessors and financiers** (they need their operators certified on time).
   - **CAMO / Part-145** organisations, OEMs, and management companies.
3. **Thought leadership.** Authoritative content on UK–EASA divergence,
   certification timelines, and common pitfalls — briefings, articles, and
   speaking at business-aviation and airline-start-up events. Positions the
   founder as *the* expert.
4. **Targeted outbound.** Direct, senior-to-senior outreach to newly announced
   start-ups, operators announcing fleet/scope expansion, and dual-market
   candidates.
5. **Investor/lessor due-diligence route.** Being the trusted name investors
   ask to vet an operator's plan — a low-cost entry that often converts into
   the full programme.

## Sales motion

- **Land with Tier 1.** A paid feasibility engagement is a low-risk first step
  for the client and a high-conversion path into a Tier 2 programme.
- **Expand to Tier 2.** Convert feasibility into the full certification
  programme.
- **Retain with Tier 3.** Convert every certificate into a compliance retainer
  at handover — designed in from day one, not sold cold later.

This **land-and-expand** motion turns a one-off certification into a multi-year
relationship and is the core of the recurring-revenue goal in Section 6.

## Proof & credibility assets

- **Anonymised case studies** and (with permission) named references.
- A crisp **methodology one-pager** (the five-phase programme) that makes the
  process legible to nervous first-time founders.
- **Regulatory-update briefings** that keep the firm in front of clients
  between engagements.

## First-year commercial priorities

1. Win a **credible anchor client** — one well-run programme is worth more than
   any marketing.
2. Build the **partner referral network** (law firms, lessors, CAMOs).
3. Publish enough **thought leadership** to be found and remembered.
4. Convert the **first retainer** to prove the recurring model.
