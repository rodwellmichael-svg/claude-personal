# 02 — Services & Offerings

Services are **productised** so that scope, deliverables, and price are clear.
Every engagement is scoped against the regulatory framework in Section 3 and
delivered using the model in Section 4.

## Tier 1 — Feasibility & Strategy

A short, fixed-fee engagement that de-risks the decision to proceed.

**Deliverables**
- Entry-route and **jurisdiction recommendation** (UK CAA vs an EASA member
  state, or both) with rationale.
- **Certification cost & timeline model** tailored to the fleet and operation.
- **Organisational gap analysis** — what people, systems, and approvals are
  missing.
- **Regulatory scope map** — the exact certificate scope, approvals, and
  specialist authorisations required.
- Go / no-go recommendation and an outline project plan.

**Typical duration:** 2–4 weeks. **Buyer:** founder / investor.

## Tier 2 — Full AOC Certification Programme (flagship)

End-to-end managed delivery from kickoff to **certificate award**.

**Workstreams**
1. **Programme management** — the five-phase plan, authority liaison, and the
   single point of accountability to the client.
2. **Management system & SMS** design and documentation.
3. **Manual suite** authoring:
   - Operations Manual **Parts A–D**
   - Management System / Compliance Monitoring documentation
   - **MEL** (from the MMEL)
   - Training & checking manuals
   - Continuing-airworthiness exposition (**CAME**) and MEL interface, via
     partner CAMO where needed
4. **Postholder / responsible-person support** — role definitions,
   recruitment support, and preparation for authority acceptance interviews.
5. **Operating Licence** support (economic authorisation) in coordination with
   legal/finance advisors.
6. **Demonstration & inspection readiness** — mock audits, proving-flight
   preparation, and base/line inspection support.

**Deliverables:** a complete, authority-accepted documentation set and a live
AOC. **Duration:** typically 6–12 months. **Pricing:** milestone-based fixed
fee plus scoped variations (see Section 6).

## Tier 3 — Post-Certification & Retained Compliance

Recurring revenue that begins the day the certificate is issued.

**Options (retainer or on-demand)**
- **Compliance monitoring** function support and independent internal audits.
- **Manual amendment** management and regulatory-change updates.
- **Scope & fleet change** projects (new type, new approval, new base).
- **Specialist approvals**: PBN, LVO, EFB, ETOPS/EDTO, RVSM, dangerous goods,
  HEMS/HHO/offshore, ACMI / wet-lease.
- **Postholder-as-a-service** — interim or fractional nominated/responsible
  persons where the authority permits and the arrangement is properly
  structured.
- **Audit readiness & mock authority audits** ahead of oversight visits.

## Specialist / add-on modules

- **Dual-AOC programmes** — running a UK and an EASA certification in parallel
  with a shared document core.
- **AOC transfer / restructuring** — ownership change, re-basing, or moving the
  principal place of business.
- **Due diligence** for investors, lessors, and lenders on an operator's
  certification status and plan.
- **Training** — bespoke workshops for client management teams on their
  post-certification obligations.

## Deliberate scope boundaries

To protect quality and manage liability, we **do not**:

- Act as the operator's legal counsel or provide legal advice (we coordinate
  with the client's lawyers).
- Sign off airworthiness we are not approved to certify (we partner with
  approved CAMOs / Part-145 organisations).
- Guarantee a certification outcome or timeline the **authority** controls —
  we manage everything within our control and are transparent about the rest.
