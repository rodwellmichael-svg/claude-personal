## 1. Revenue model

Three stacked revenue streams, each with a different rhythm:

| Stream | Pricing basis | Cash rhythm |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Tier 1 — Feasibility & Strategy** | Fixed fee | Fast, up-front, low value, high volume |
| **Tier 2 — Certification Programme** | Milestone-based fixed fee (+ scoped variations) | Large, over 6–12 months |
| **Tier 3 — Retained Compliance** | Monthly / annual retainer + day-rate overflow | Recurring, sticky, covers overhead |

**Design goal:** Tier 3 recurring revenue should grow to **cover fixed
overhead**, so the business is not forced to win certification work just to keep
the lights on. That is the difference between a stable firm and a feast-or-
famine consultancy.

## 3. Cost structure

**Fixed (low by design):**
- Core salaries (kept minimal — see Section 5)
- Professional indemnity & other insurance (material for this sector)
- Tooling / document-management / CRM
- Accounting, legal, regulatory subscriptions
- Marketing & brand

**Variable (scales with revenue):**
- Associate fees (the largest COGS line) — engaged per project
- Travel to client sites and authorities
- Project-specific specialist input

**Target gross margin on managed programmes: 45–60%** once the associate model
is running — the spread between the fixed fee charged and the associate day-
rates paid, net of core-team delivery time.

## 5. Working capital & funding

- Certification programmes span months; **milestone billing with a meaningful
  deposit** is essential to avoid funding the client's project from our balance
  sheet.
- Hold a **working-capital buffer** for the gap between milestones and for slow
  quarters (demand is lumpy).
- The business is **capital-light**: it can plausibly be **self-funded /
  bootstrapped** by a credible founder with a first anchor client, avoiding
  external equity. Model both a bootstrapped and a modestly-funded case.

## 6. Key financial risks

- **Fixed-fee overrun** — a programme that balloons in scope destroys its
  margin. Mitigate with tight scoping, explicit assumptions, and a **variation
  process** for anything outside scope.
- **Concentration** — over-reliance on one large client. Diversify the pipeline.
- **Bad debt** — an under-capitalised operator that fails mid-programme.
  Mitigate with qualification, deposits, and milestone billing.
- **Key-person dependency** — early revenue rides on the founder. Mitigate by
  building the second senior consultant and the associate bench early.
