# 00 — Executive Summary

## The venture

**AeroCert Partners** is a specialist aviation regulatory consultancy that
takes operators from concept to a live **UK CAA** or **EASA** Air Operator
Certificate (AOC), and then keeps them compliant afterwards. We sell the
scarce thing every new operator needs and few can hire full-time: senior,
authority-facing certification expertise, packaged as a managed project.

An AOC programme is long (typically **6–12 months** for a straightforward
operation, longer for complex fleets), document-heavy, and unforgiving of
error. Delays burn a start-up's cash while aircraft, crews, and leases sit
idle. Our value proposition is simple: **we compress the timeline and de-risk
the outcome.**

## The problem we solve

A new commercial operator must simultaneously:

- Choose the right **State of the operator / principal place of business**
  (post-Brexit, a UK AOC and an EASA AOC are now two separate certificates
  with two separate regulators).
- Build a compliant **management system and safety management system (SMS)**.
- Write a full suite of **manuals** (Operations Manual Parts A–D, Management
  System / Compliance Monitoring, MEL, training manuals, and the continuing
  airworthiness exposition).
- Recruit and get authority **acceptance of nominated/responsible persons**.
- Pass a **five-phase certification process** ending in on-site inspections
  and demonstration flights.
- In parallel, secure an **Operating Licence** (economic authorisation) and
  the necessary **airworthiness and CAMO** approvals.

Founders rarely have all of this in-house. We provide it as a service.

## What we sell

Three productised tiers (detailed in Section 2):

1. **Feasibility & Strategy** — entry route, jurisdiction selection, cost and
   timeline model, gap analysis.
2. **Full AOC Certification Programme** — end-to-end managed delivery to
   certificate award, including manuals, postholder support, and authority
   liaison.
3. **Post-Certification & Retained Compliance** — ongoing compliance
   monitoring, audits, manual amendments, scope/fleet changes, and
   "postholder-as-a-service."

## Why us / why now

- **Regulatory divergence.** UK–EU divergence since Brexit created a genuine
  new market: operators wanting access to both markets increasingly need
  **both** a UK and an EASA AOC, doubling the certification workload.
- **Fragmented supply.** Expertise sits with a handful of ex-authority
  individuals and a few large consultancies. A focused, senior, fixed-scope
  boutique is well positioned between the freelancer and the incumbents.
- **Sticky revenue.** Certification is a one-off, but compliance is forever.
  Every AOC we deliver seeds a retained relationship.

## The ask / the model

The business is **capital-light and expertise-heavy**. It can be launched by a
credible founder plus a curated **associate network**, with modest working
capital to cover the gap between project milestones. See Section 6 for the
financial model and Section 9 for the 0–18 month roadmap.

## Headline targets (illustrative — see Section 6)

- **Year 1:** 2–4 certification programmes + 3–5 feasibility/retained clients.
- **Year 3:** 8–12 concurrent engagements, a bench of 8–12 associates, and a
  recurring retained-compliance book covering fixed overheads.
- **Gross margin target:** 45–60% on managed programmes once the associate
  model is running.
